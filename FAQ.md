# Stormring FAQ

Your question is not here? [Ask it, or report a bug or idea](https://github.com/acetousk/stormring/issues/new/choose).

## Questions

**How do I get a gun?**
Hold **Use** (E, X on a gamepad, or USE on touch) on a chest. You get a rifle and a heal kit.

**How do I build a wall?**
First hit trees, rocks, or houses with the pickaxe to get material. Then press **Q** (Y, or BUILD). A green wall means you can build there. A purple wall means you can't.

**Why do I lose health when nobody shoots me?**
You are outside the circle. The purple storm hurts anyone outside, and it hurts more as it shrinks. Run to the middle.

**Can I play with friends?**
Yes, up to 4. One player picks **Host** and sends the 4-digit code. The others pick **Join** and type it. See "Known problems" first.

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. The web version runs in Chrome, Firefox, or Edge. For Android, Windows, and Linux, see [Play](README.md#play).

## Known problems

**Host and Join do not work in the web, phone, or PC version.**
The online keys are not set up yet. Play **Solo** for now. Online works only from the Godot editor.

**The web page shows an old version.**
Press **Ctrl+Shift+R** on the game page.
