# Stormring FAQ

Your question is not here? [Ask it, or report a bug or idea](https://github.com/acetousk/stormring/issues/new/choose).

## Questions

**Are there guns?**
No. It is a food fight. You throw tomatoes, and a hit is a splat.

**How do I get tomatoes?**
Hold **Use** (E, X on a gamepad, or USE on touch) on a lunch box. You get tomatoes and a napkin.

**My tomatoes land short. Why?**
Tomatoes fall as they fly, like a real ball. The game aims the arc at the dot, but a moving target may step away. Throw a bit ahead of a runner.

**How do I build a wall?**
First tap a tray cart with the spoon to get trays. Then press **Q** (Y, or BUILD). A green ghost wall means you can build there. A purple wall means you can't.

**Why do I get messy when nobody throws at me?**
You are outside the circle. The green jello spill makes anyone outside messy, and more as it closes in. Run to the middle.

**What does "splatted" mean?**
Your clean meter reached 0%. You are out of this round. Watch the rest, then play again.

**Can I play with friends?**
Yes, up to 4. One player picks **Host** and sends the 4-digit code. The others pick **Join** and type it. See "Known problems" first.

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. The web version runs in Chrome, Firefox, or Edge. For Android, Windows, and Linux, see [Play](README.md#play).

## Known problems

**Host and Join do not work in the web, phone, or PC version.**
The online keys are not set up yet. Play **Solo** for now. Online works only from the Godot editor.

**The web page shows an old version.**
Press **Ctrl+Shift+R** on the game page.
